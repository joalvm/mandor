# Trademarks

The code is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only](LICENSE). The
names and logos are not: the license covers the code, and section 7(e) of the AGPL lets a project
decline to grant rights over its trademarks. This one does.

## The marks

- The names **Mandor**, **Mandor Realtime** and **Mandor Request**.
- The rocket logo, in every color, and the app icons in `apps/*/resources/`.

They belong to Alejandro Vilchez. They identify the builds published from this repository, so
that anyone who installs "Mandor" knows who made it.

## What you can do without asking

- Say what the project is: "Mandor Request supports OpenAPI", "a plugin for Mandor Realtime",
  "a fork of Mandor".
- Link to the project and to its releases, and show its logo next to that link.
- Redistribute the official builds unmodified, under their own names.
- Write about it, review it, teach with it.

## What needs another name

A modified version, a fork, or any build that does not come from this repository:

- uses its own name, which does not contain "Mandor" or anything confusingly close to it;
- replaces the logo and every app icon;
- does not suggest that it is official, endorsed, or made by this project.

The same applies to domain names, app store listings and package names.
