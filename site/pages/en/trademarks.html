The names of Mandor

The license covers the code, not the names and logos: section 7(e) of the AGPL lets a project decline to grant rights over its trademarks, and this one does. The names Mandor, Mandor Realtime and Mandor Request, the rocket logo in every color and the app icons belong to Alejandro Vilchez. They tell anyone who installs Mandor who made it.

The full policy is TRADEMARKS.md, and every installer carries a copy.

What you can do without asking

  • Say what the project is: Mandor Request supports OpenAPI, a plugin for Mandor Realtime, a fork of Mandor.
  • Link to the project and its releases, with its logo next to that link.
  • Redistribute the official builds unchanged, under their own names.
  • Write about it, review it, teach with it.

What needs another name

A changed version, a fork, or any build that does not come from this repository:

  • takes its own name, without Mandor or anything confusingly close;
  • replaces the logo and every app icon;
  • does not suggest it is official, endorsed or made by this project.

The same goes for domain names, app store listings and package names. For anything this page does not cover, open an issue first.
